Our Story

Meet Tiffany

Hair Style by Tiffany started as a dream in a small home studio. Over 8 years, it's grown into Topeka's go-to destination for protective styling and natural hair care.

Founded by Tiffany, a licensed cosmetologist with a deep passion for braiding, our salon is built on three pillars: precision, education, and love for natural hair. Every client leaves feeling beautiful, confident, and understood.

We specialize in protective styles that promote hair growth while keeping you stunning. From first-time braiders to seasoned loc wearers — everyone is welcome here.
